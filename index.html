NAVYA
Institute Operating System
Foundation build started

One digital system for the complete institute.

NAVYA is being built as a secure multi-tenant platform for schools, coaching institutes, colleges, universities and training centres — combining academics, exams, administration, communication, analytics and AI.

🏫 Organizations

Separate workspace, branding, users and data for every institute.

👩‍🏫 Teachers

Classes, questions, tests, assignments, content and teacher workspace.

🎓 Students

Learning, exams, results, attendance, homework and progress.

📚 Library

Private → department → institute → group → central → marketplace.

📝 Exams

Question Bank, Test Builder, Paper Designer, online exams and results.

🤖 AI

Question generation, extraction, solutions, analytics and assistant tools.

📊 Analytics

Student, teacher and institute-level performance insights.

💼 Operations

Attendance, admissions, communication, finance, HR and documents.

Institute Videos

Video support is part of NAVYA's architecture.

Initial approach: institutes can add supported external video links (such as YouTube) and NAVYA will display them inside the institute's video area. NAVYA does not need to store every video itself.

Future option: direct video upload/storage can be enabled as a premium feature when required.
